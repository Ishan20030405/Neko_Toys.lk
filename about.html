About Neko Toys

Sri Lanka's premier destination for authentic anime figures, cosplay items, mangas & merchandise.

Our Story

Neko Toys was born from a simple passion — a love for anime and the incredible worlds it creates. Founded in 2020 in Colombo, Sri Lanka, we started as a small online store with a handful of figures and a big dream.

What began in a spare room has grown into one of Sri Lanka's most loved anime merchandise stores, shipping authentic, high-quality figures and collectibles to fans all across the island.

Every product we carry is hand-picked by our team of anime enthusiasts. We know the difference between a quality scale figure and a cheap knock-off — and we only stock the best.
